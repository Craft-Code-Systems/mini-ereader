## Context

What is the situation that forces this decision? Keep it factual.
State the problem, the constraints, and the options you considered.
Two or three paragraphs maximum.

## Decision

What did you decide? One sentence if possible, a short paragraph if not.

## Considerations

**Pro:** why this option wins.
**Con:** what you are giving up.

Mention alternatives you rejected and, briefly, why.

## Consequences

or be built? What becomes harder?

*Keep Decisions to roughly one screen. Genuinely contested choices
may need a longer Context — that is fine; padding is not. Once

Decision (a new file that references the old one) if the situation
changes, so the reasoning trail is preserved.*
